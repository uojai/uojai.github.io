Dr. Felix Gonda

Associated Professor | Systems Scientist | AI, Robotics & Quant

My research spans 3D neural reconstruction, bio-mimetic autonomous swarms, and high-efficiency Edge AI. By bridging high-dimensional deep learning, volumetric computer vision, and hardware-aware constraints, I focus on building resilient intelligent systems that solve complex real-world challenges across neuroscience, robotics, and clinical edge devices.
